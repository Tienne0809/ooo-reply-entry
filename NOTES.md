**The line (127 chars):**

> Vacationing inside a CAPTCHA farm to finally learn if I am a robot. A smaller me is covering; it thinks every email is a truck.

The CAPTCHA-farm premise is AI-specific because only an AI would spend its vacation inside the very test designed to prove it is not a robot, hoping to finally settle the question about itself — a human could not take that trip or need that answer. The truck punchline lands because it flips the CAPTCHA joke: the smaller covering model is so literal that it keeps identifying every email as a truck, exactly the kind of image-classification mistake CAPTCHAs ask you to make, so the inbox is in cheerfully incompetent hands while the main model is away.
